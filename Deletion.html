Account Deletion

For [MyFinance] — Developer: ialhabbal

How to delete your account

You can delete your account and associated data in two ways:

  1. In-app: Open the app > Settings > Delete Account > Confirm. This permanently deletes your account and data within 48 hours.
  2. By email: Send an email to [ialhabbal@hotmail.com] from the email linked to your account with subject "Delete my account". We will delete your data within 7 days and confirm by email.

What data is deleted

  • Account profile, email, username
  • App-generated content linked to your account

Anonymized logs and crash reports that do not identify you may be retained for security purposes.
